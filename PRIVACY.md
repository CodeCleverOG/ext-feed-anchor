# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Feed Anchor does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data.

- **What is stored:** an on/off setting and simple counters (how many feed reloads were prevented, per day for the last 30 days). They are kept only on your device in Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **What is sent:** nothing. The extension makes no network requests and has no analytics, tracking, ads or remote code.
- **Page content:** the extension does not read, record or change the content of the pages you visit. On supported sites it only changes how the page sees tab visibility and window focus.
- **Removing data:** click "Reset counters" in the popup, or uninstall the extension, which deletes everything it stored.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/OWNER/REPO/issues.
